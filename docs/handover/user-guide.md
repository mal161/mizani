# User guide

Short task guides for each kind of user. Replace `mizani.acc.example` with the ACC's address.

## Complainant

**File a complaint:** go to `mizani.acc.example/en`, choose **File a complaint**, enter your mobile number and the 6-digit code we text you. Give the advocate's name (pick them from the list if they appear), what happened, and attach up to 5 documents or photos. You get a case number and a 6-digit PIN by SMS. Keep the PIN private.

**Follow your case:** sign in again with your phone to see every update, or check the status without signing in at **Track a case** (case number + PIN). From a basic phone, dial the ACC USSD code and enter the case number and PIN.

**When we ask for more information:** you get an SMS; sign in, read the request on your case and reply (you can attach documents).

**Hearing and decision:** the case page shows the hearing date, time and place (or the online link). After the decision you see the outcome and reasons and can download the decision letter.

**No phone or no internet?** Visit an ACC office: staff will file the complaint for you and print a slip with your case number and PIN.

## Mlalamikaji

**Kuwasilisha lalamiko:** nenda `mizani.acc.example/sw`, chagua **Wasilisha lalamiko**, weka nambari yako ya simu na nambari ya tarakimu 6 tutakayokutumia kwa SMS. Andika jina la wakili, kilichotokea, na uambatishe hadi nyaraka au picha 5. Utapokea nambari ya kesi na PIN ya tarakimu 6 kwa SMS. Usimpe mtu PIN yako.

**Kufuatilia kesi:** ingia tena kwa simu yako kuona kila taarifa, au angalia hali bila kuingia kupitia **Fuatilia kesi** (nambari ya kesi + PIN). Kwa simu ya kawaida, piga nambari ya USSD ya ACC kisha weka nambari ya kesi na PIN.

**Tukiomba maelezo zaidi:** utapokea SMS; ingia, soma ombi kwenye kesi yako na ujibu (unaweza kuambatisha nyaraka).

**Usikilizaji na uamuzi:** ukurasa wa kesi unaonyesha tarehe, saa na mahali pa usikilizaji (au kiungo cha mtandaoni). Baada ya uamuzi utaona matokeo na sababu, na unaweza kupakua barua ya uamuzi.

**Huna simu au intaneti?** Tembelea ofisi ya ACC: wafanyakazi watawasilisha lalamiko kwa niaba yako na kukuchapishia risiti yenye nambari ya kesi na PIN.

## Advocate

**Activate your account:** the notice letter served on you contains an activation code. Go to `mizani.acc.example/en/advocate/activate`, enter your admission number, the activation code and the mobile number on the roll, then the SMS code.

**Respond:** your case page shows the allegations, the complainant's name, the documents the ACC shared with you, and your response deadline. Write your response and attach documents before the deadline; later responses are accepted but marked late. You can add further responses.

**Hearing and decision:** the case page shows hearing details and, later, the decision with reasons and a downloadable letter.

## Wakili

**Kuanzisha akaunti:** barua ya ilani uliyopewa ina nambari ya uanzishaji. Nenda `mizani.acc.example/sw/advocate/activate`, weka nambari yako ya usajili (admission), nambari ya uanzishaji na nambari ya simu iliyo kwenye orodha ya mawakili, kisha nambari ya SMS.

**Kujibu:** ukurasa wa kesi unaonyesha madai, jina la mlalamikaji, nyaraka ambazo ACC imekushirikisha, na tarehe ya mwisho ya kujibu. Andika jibu lako na uambatishe nyaraka kabla ya tarehe hiyo; majibu ya baadaye yanapokelewa lakini yanaonyeshwa kuwa yamechelewa.

**Usikilizaji na uamuzi:** ukurasa wa kesi unaonyesha maelezo ya usikilizaji na, baadaye, uamuzi pamoja na sababu na barua ya kupakua.

## Officer

- **Sign in** at `/en/staff/login` with email, password and the authenticator code.
- **Workspace:** tabs *My cases*, *Unassigned*, *Overdue*, *All*; search by case number or name.
- **Walk-in intake:** **Walk-in intake** → fill in the complainant's details and complaint → print the slip.
- **Work a case:** set category and priority (AI may suggest; click **Apply suggestion** to use it), assign, add internal notes, request information from the complainant, move the stage.
- **Notify the advocate:** in the Advocate panel, match the advocate on the roll, tick the documents to share, record how and when the notice was served, download and serve the notice letter.
- **Hearings:** in the Hearing panel, set date/time, length, venue or online link and the panel; confirm if Mizani warns that a commissioner is already booked. Adjourn or cancel with a reason (both parties see it).
- **Referrals:** after a tribunal referral, record when it was lodged and the tribunal's reference; download the referral pack.

## Administrator

Everything an officer does, plus **Administration**: staff accounts (create, deactivate, change role), workflow settings (stage names, deadlines, response window, AI switch), routing rules, and the audit log with CSV export. Administrators can also use the Commission portal.

## Commissioner

- **Sign in** like staff; you land on **Commission**: your upcoming hearings, cases awaiting a decision, recent decisions; **Hearings** shows the week's agenda.
- **Before a hearing:** open the case and **Download case bundle** (one PDF with the complaint, responses, history and evidence; confidential).
- **After the hearing:** record the minutes (internal), then the decision: outcome, amount for refunds, reasons (published to both parties), orders and panel. A correction replaces the published decision and keeps the earlier one on record.
- **Analytics:** resolution times, where cases wait, complaint trends, officer workload, advocates with repeated complaints and decision outcomes, by period and office, each with CSV download.
